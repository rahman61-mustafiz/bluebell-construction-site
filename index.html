05 / Questions

Questions owners ask us.

Straight answers before you sign anything.

Will the final cost match the quote?

You get a full BOQ before work starts. If something needs to change, we price it and you approve it first.

What if the work runs late?

The schedule is agreed with you at the start. If anything moves it, you hear it from us early, with the reason and the new date.

Do you follow the building code?

Yes. Our design and site work follow BNBC, and each stage is checked before the next one starts.

Can you handle approvals?

Yes. We prepare the drawings and the paperwork for approvals as part of the design stage.

Do you take small jobs?

Yes. Repairs, boundary walls and fit-outs get the same care as a full building.
